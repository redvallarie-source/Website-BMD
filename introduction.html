About Our Department

Department of Business Management

Cultivating ethical leaders for a better world

The Department of Business Management at Tzu Chi University combines solid academic foundation with humanistic values, preparing students to become innovative, responsible, and compassionate professionals in a global society.

Our Journey

History of Department

1997

Established as Department of Accounting

Founded with the aim of cultivating students with professional accounting knowledge and skills.

2002

Department of Accounting Information

Renamed to respond to the market demand for accounting and information integration talents, adding information management courses.

2013

Major Transformation & Renaming

Renamed to Department of Marketing and Circulation Management. The focus shifted to marketing strategy, logistics management, and circulation to enhance students' employment competitiveness.

Core Competencies

Cultivate Four Distinctive Talents

Operations Management

Focus on effective management of business operations and industry analysis capabilities.

Smart Business

Involves the use of business intelligence and technology to enhance business processes.

Sustainable Innovation

Emphasis on sustainable management concepts, social innovation, and workplace ethics.

Culinary Management

Covers the management aspects of cooking, food service, and sustainable innovative culinary.

Building cross-cultural perspective and global readiness through three main pillars:

  • 1
